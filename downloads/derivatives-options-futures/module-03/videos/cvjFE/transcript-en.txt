From the watch list
on the monitor tab, click the underlying
symbol you wish to create your options trade for. So long as your tabs are part
of the same Windows group, the underlying will appear
in the order entry panel. To the right of the
order entry panel, select option chain from
the drop-down menu. The option chain display, shows call options to the left, and put options to the right of the central strike column. You will see the price
and the daily change of the underlying to the
upper right of the window. Above the call area, you will see tabs for
different explorations. Click a tab or to locate
additional exploration dates, click on the, "More" tab. Above the port area, you will see a drop down
menu for strike prices. Access this menu to change or refine the number of strike
prices displayed on the page. Hovering the cursor above any bid price displays
a red background. This indicates that by
clicking on the field, it would create an order
to sell that option. Hovering above any ask price, displays a blue background indicating that by
clicking on this field, it would create an
audit to buy an option. Locate the design option
strike you want to transact, click on the relevant
price field, which will close the
option chain display. Use the order entry panel to define the quantity
of options to trade. Choose the right order type
from the drop down menu. If you're using a limit price, choose the value by clicking in the input field or simply
typing in the value. Select from the time
and force drop down whether this is a
day or a GTC order. Your order is ready to review. Click the, "Submit" button to generate an order confirmation. When you have reviewed the
details, click, "Transmit." The order is sent for execution at the exchange and will appear in the activity panel to
the lower left of mosaic. To pause or interact
with an act of order. Click on it in the activity panel and make the relevant choice.
