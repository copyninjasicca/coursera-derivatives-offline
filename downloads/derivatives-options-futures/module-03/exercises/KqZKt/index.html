Question 1

An investor pays $50.00 for a stock and writes a call option at the $55.00 strike which expires in 30 days. The investor receives a $2.00 premium for the call option. What is the overall gain should the option expire when the stock settles at $60.00?

Question 2

An investor receives a $2.00 premium for a call option with a $30.00 strike price and pays $27.50 for shares in company XYZ Corp. When the call option expires the stock has decreased to $25.00. What is the profit or loss on the covered call strategy at expiration?

Question 3

An investor buys shares and sells a call option $10.00 out-of-the-money. What is the profit on a covered call strategy at expiration when the call option ends $10.00 in-the-money for which the investor received a premium of $5.00?
