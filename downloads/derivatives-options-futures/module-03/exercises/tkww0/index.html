Question 1

An investor sells a stock for $50.00 and writes a put option at the $45.00 strike which expires in 30 days. The investor receives a $3.00 premium for the put option. What is the overall gain should the option expire when the stock settles at $40.00?

Question 2

An investor receives a $2.00 premium for a put option with a $30.00 strike price and sells shares in company XYZ Corp at $37.50. When the put option expires the stock has increased to $45.00. What is the profit or loss on the covered put strategy at expiration?

Question 3

An investor sells shares and sells a put option $10.00 out-of-the-money. What is the profit on a covered put strategy at expiration when the put option ends $10.00 in-the-money for which the investor received a premium of $5.00?
