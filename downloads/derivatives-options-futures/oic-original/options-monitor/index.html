Options Monitor

Original reference archive. The original interactive calculator is not operational offline.

With the Options Monitor, powered by iVolatility, explore quotes for stocks, ETFs and indexes, while viewing a variety of key options data, including Delta, implied volatility, bid-ask spreads and volume. Expirations can be changed to display additional data, including LEAPS®, Weeklys and Quarterlies. Plus, access to the Options Calculator and Probability Calculator is available directly from within the monitor, simply by clicking the calculator icon in the Strike column. Note that all data for US stocks, options, dividends and interest rates are provided with a 20-minute delay. OCC is providing this tool for educational purposes only. OCC makes no representation as to the timeliness, accuracy or validity of the information and this information should not be construed as a recommendation to purchase or sell a security, or to provide investment advice. For more information view this help file or video this video tutorial. For further questions, contact us at options@theocc.com. While this tool is accessible on various devices, it is designed with specific features and functionalities that are optimized for desktop usage.
