Options Monitor

With the Options Monitor, powered by iVolatility, explore quotes for stocks, ETFs and indexes, while viewing a variety of key options data, including Delta, implied volatility, bid-ask spreads and volume. Expirations can be changed to display additional data, including LEAPS®, Weeklys and Quarterlies. Plus, access to the Options Calculator and Probability Calculator is available directly from within the monitor, simply by clicking the calculator icon in the Strike column. Note that all data for US stocks, options, dividends and interest rates are provided with a 20-minute delay.
