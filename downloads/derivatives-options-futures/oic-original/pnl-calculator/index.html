Position Profit & Loss Simulator

Original reference archive. The original interactive calculator is not operational offline.

The P&L Simulator, powered by iVolatility, allows you to explore the potential profit and loss of hypothetical trades. Customize variables such as stock price, volatility, interest rates, days to expiration and dividends to model theoretical results. Note that all data for US stocks, options, dividends and interest rates are provided with a 20-minute delay. OCC is providing this tool for educational purposes only. OCC makes no representation as to the timeliness, accuracy or validity of the information and this information should not be construed as a recommendation to purchase or sell a security, or to provide investment advice. For more information view these help files. For further questions, contact us at options@theocc.com. While our tool is accessible on various devices, we have designed it with specific features and functionalities that are optimized for desktop usage.

Functions and dependencies

Multiple stock and option legs, quantity, side, entry price, theoretical P&L, aggregate Greeks and scenario charts; relative/absolute IV shifts.

Remote option chain and valuation services. Original UI was observed online; the complete application and server engine are not supplied.

Official guide: Black-Scholes for European and certain no-dividend American cases; otherwise Cox–Ross–Rubinstein tree. Tree step count is not specified. Position Greeks scale with quantity and contract multiplier; default option multiplier is 100.

What was verified

Original option chain and position editor loaded; adding a hypothetical 100-share stock leg at entry price 100 produced a 10,000 debit and 100% displayed Delta. This is a basic UI/arithmetic observation, not an option valuation equivalence test.
