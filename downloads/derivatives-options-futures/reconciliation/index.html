Models and verification

The existing BSM/arithmetic suite passed 188 checks; the American suite passed 91 checks; the probability suite passed 1003 checks; the portfolio/import/HV suite passed 16 test groups. Groups and individual checks are separate units. These tests support the documented models, rather than vendor identity.

American validation uses QuantLib reference examples, exercise bounds, convergence, numerical Greeks, IV round trips and cash timing. Probability validation includes independent first-exit quadrature, a seeded Brownian-bridge Monte Carlo check, absorbing-barrier series convergence and event identities. Position tests use hand-calculated signed costs/expiry payoffs, BSM Greeks, IV-shift units, American integration, cash-event timing, strict imports and independently computed HV.

Local time is ACT/365 with fractional calendar days. Theta is per day; vega and rho are per one percentage point; option values are per share. Positions scale by signed quantity and multiplier. Tree Greeks may oscillate with step count. Cash at expiry is excluded, and scheduled payments are shifted by elapsed scenario time. Immediate exercise can make implied volatility non-unique.

Original pricing reconciliation

Three earlier European cases and one American case, plus 5 fresh cases, were captured from the authenticated original UI. Every original pricing/Greek value has four displayed decimals: tolerance is 0.00005, unchanged across models. The original stock input used a zero regular cash dividend; mapping that to q=0 is an assumption. The guide names a 100-step binomial American model but does not establish its exact construction or Greek bumps. Raising local CRR steps does not remove all differences; no fitted adjustment is applied. Fresh zero-rate one-year and fractional-day cases also have residual differences. The half-day case produces positive time value, so this observation does not support simple truncation of hours. Exact vendor time/discount/volatility conventions remain unknown. The fresh zero-time case returns correct intrinsic prices (5/0), while its Alpha and Rho displays are nonzero; their boundary semantics or stale-output behavior is unresolved. Local BSM boundary Greeks are explicitly undefined and are not treated as numeric matches.
