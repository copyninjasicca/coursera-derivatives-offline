[MUSIC] In this module we're going to get technical, as we teach you about the mechanics behind the options market. Some investors may be satisfied knowing that call options are associated with rising prices. And that put options are associated with falling prices. But behind the scenes there's a lot going on with how option prices move. We'll start the module with a note about some algebraic logic behind options known as, put/call parity. And then we're going to take a deep dive into what factors are important in determining the price of an option. We'll also help you visualize the payout of an option trade with respect to the price of the underlying asset. And finally, we'll walk you through a series of risk variables known as option Greeks. These values are important since they tell investors how much or how little the value of an option will change with respect to changes in price, time, or volatility. Let's get started. [MUSIC]
